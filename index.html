Star Field Generator

Computes star fields as seen by either the HST, JWST or GMT telescope.

Camera

Sets the imaging camera photometric filter, pixel scale, field-of-view and exposure time.

Stars

Sets the star spatial and brightness random distributions.

The spatial distribution of the stars is either uniform (x,y) or globular ((r,theta) where r and theta follow a Cauchy and Uniform distributions, respectively).

The star magnitudes are drawn according to a Normal distribution.
